THE MARGIN-VERSUS-SPREAD ORDERING IS THE POINT

The earlier G2 pass was a 19,260 KiB margin against a ~165,000 KiB spread — a
coin flip that later landed the other way and regressed to FAIL. A reader given
only a median cannot tell that case from this one. The block therefore states
both quantities and their ordering, and the *sentence* is generated from the
peaks rather than written: `g2_robustness_prose` selects among three branches —
margin wider than spread, margin narrower than spread, or median over the
ceiling — so a weaker future measurement produces weaker prose instead of an
unchanged boast. The superseded measurement is cited by path as the shape being
avoided, with its own spread (164,552 KiB) and excess (13,692 KiB, FAIL) also
derived from its artefact.

HOW THE TEST DERIVES RATHER THAN RESTATES

`readme_publishes_the_newest_measured_memory_figures_not_a_remembered_one`
reads two live artifacts and computes everything else:

  * ceilings — `oc_testkit::perf::FrozenThresholds::from_baseline(
    oc_testkit::perf::load_committed_baseline())`, i.e. `benchmarks/ts-baseline.json`,
    never a literal; and
  * the five per-repetition Rust peaks — from the newest committed artefact,
    **discovered** by scanning `.omo/evidence/task-<n>-opencode-rust.txt` and
    taking the highest task number that parses as a G1/G2 measurement, so a
    later re-measurement becomes the source of truth the moment it is committed.

Median, margin, excess, spread, ratio, margin-percent, per-run ceiling
violations and verdict are all methods on `GateFigures`. The artefact's own
narrative is used only as a cross-check, and where it disagrees with its own
table the derived value wins and the test fails:

  * the ceiling the artefact prints must equal `0.50 x` the committed baseline
    median (catches an artefact measured against a different baseline);
  * the median the artefact restates in two places must equal the median of its
    own five peaks; and
  * the verdict the artefact records must equal the verdict its own median
    implies against the frozen ceiling.

MUTATION PROOF — FOUR INDEPENDENT PERTURBATIONS, FOUR FAILURES

  1. README digit. `19,472 KiB margin` -> `19,473 KiB margin`, artefact
     untouched. FAILED: "README.md block `memory-gate-measurement` is stale".
     Restored; passes.

  2. Artefact peak, README untouched. Task 123's `1,510,528` -> `1,520,528`.
     FAILED, and the *judgement* changed with the data: the regenerated prose
     read "1.29% of the ceiling — is **narrower than** the 27,032 KiB five-run
     spread", i.e. the conclusion tracks the measurement rather than the
     wording. Restored; passes.

  3. A newer artefact wins. A synthetic `task-999-opencode-rust.txt` with a
     different median made the test demand a block citing
     `.omo/evidence/task-999-opencode-rust.txt`, proving discovery-by-newest is
     real and not a hardcoded task-123 path. Removed; passes.

  4. The contradiction guard itself.
     `a_measurement_whose_prose_contradicts_its_own_peaks_is_rejected` feeds a
     forged artefact whose W-real peaks are 90,000,000 KiB while its prose
     claims PASS. It panics with "the artefact records `PASS` but its own median
     90000000 KiB against the frozen 1513496 KiB ceiling is a FAIL", so the
     derived side demonstrably dominates the narrated side. This is a
     `#[should_panic]` test, not an `#[ignore]`.

The forged-artefact ceilings in (4) are themselves formatted from
`FrozenThresholds`, so that test cannot drift from the committed baseline
either.

ONE ADDITIONAL STALE FIGURE FOUND AND FIXED

`README.md` claimed "Twenty-three currently have local backends; the other 35
return an operation-specific `503 backend_unavailable`". The two counts were
**inverted**: `docs/compatibility-matrix.md`'s generated `api-operations` block
contains exactly 35 `implemented` rows and 23 `explicit gap (503 backend
unavailable)` rows, and its prose says "Thirty-five have local backends; 23
explicit 503 backend gaps". The README and the matrix therefore contradicted each
other after todos 127/128 backed 22 more operations.

Rather than retype the corrected pair, both halves are now derived from the same
route probe the matrix block already runs. `assert_api_counts` gained:

  backed  = upstream operations that are served and not 503
  gapped  = upstream operations that are served and 503
  assert backed + gapped + missing == upstream.len()

and then requires `README.md` to contain "all 58 upstream operations",
"35 of the 58 upstream operations have local backends", and "the remaining 23
return an". Mutation proof: restoring the inverted wording fails with
`README.md must mention "35 of the 58 upstream operations have local backends"`.
