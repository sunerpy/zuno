Task 156 — send and decode the real Responses shape on Responses surfaces
Closes F4's wave-9 blocker 2.

THE DEFECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
`CompatibleProvider` selected `/responses` for Responses surfaces but still used
the Chat Completions body and decoder on the wire. The request always contained
`messages`, and the stream translator only understood `choices[].delta`. The
production Azure/Copilot tests hid this by mounting a Chat cassette on a
Responses endpoint.

THE FIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
`RequestBody::build` now dispatches on the already-resolved `ApiSurface`:

- Chat surfaces retain `messages`, Chat-form tools, and the existing quirks.
- Responses surfaces emit `input`, Responses-form tools, and
  `max_output_tokens`.
- Reasoning, tool calls/results, images, and protected reasoning content are
  mapped into their Responses wire forms without changing provider identity,
  endpoint, URL, or header selection.

`CompatibleProvider::stream` resolves the same surface before decoding and
uses `SurfaceTranslator`. Chat continues through `ChunkTranslator`; Responses
uses `ResponsesTranslator`, which handles typed `response.*` text, reasoning,
function-call, terminal, usage, `[DONE]`, and wire-error events.

The production replay helpers now assert the request discriminant. Azure and
Copilot `gpt-5` Responses routes replay
`openai-responses/gpt-5-5-streams-text`; Chat routes continue to replay Chat
recordings. No Chat cassette remains mounted as evidence for those Responses
routes.

MUTATION PROOF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Forced a Responses surface through the Chat request builder.
   `responses_surface_uses_input_and_max_output_tokens_not_chat_fields` failed.
2. Forced a Responses surface through the Chat stream decoder.
   `responses_events_decode_text_reasoning_tools_and_usage` failed.

Both mutations were restored before the final gates.
