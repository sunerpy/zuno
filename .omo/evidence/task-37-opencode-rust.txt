Stale-handle abort proof:
1. Create the control handle before turn N.
2. End turn N and start turn N+1 with a new live interrupt signal.
3. Wait until turn N+1's provider stream is hanging.
4. Invoke `abort()` through the old control handle.
5. Observed: `abort() == true`, the turn returns `TurnOutcome::Interrupted`, `TurnInterrupted` is emitted, and turn N+1's live signal is set. Completion is bounded by a 2-second timeout, proving abort wakes the in-flight provider stream rather than depending on consumer polling.
