9. DEVIATION FROM THE PROMPT, STATED
------------------------------------
The real-backup test is gated on the env var OPENCODE_LEGACY_DB rather than on the
file merely existing. Reason: it copies 2.6 GB and takes 35 s, which every future
`cargo test --workspace` would pay. The skip is loud, not silent — it prints the
env var name, the measured path, whether that path is present, and why it is
opt-in. I ran it myself with the variable set; section 4 is that run.
