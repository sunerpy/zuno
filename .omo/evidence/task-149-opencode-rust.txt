Structural contract
-------------------
- The production support matrix must be generated from `HookName::ALL` and map through an
  exhaustive `match HookName`; adding a variant without a production mapping must fail to
  compile or fail the matrix equality test.
- Documentation rows must render from the same matrix, not maintain a second handwritten
  list.
- Hook dispatch remains sequential in configured plugin order. Every mutable output passed
  to the next plugin is the previous plugin's output, and production consumes the final value.
