Delegate a self-contained unit of work to the named child `agent` in its own session.
Provide the required typed contract: `objective`, `deliverable`, `instructions`, and `success_evidence`. Use `scope.include`/`scope.exclude`, `constraints.must`/`constraints.must_not`, and `dependencies` when they materially bound or enable the work.
The child model, reasoning, MCP, Skill configuration, and sandbox inherit from the parent composition and are narrowed by the selected Agent's policy.
The parent retains integration decisions and final verification. Do not duplicate the delegated work in the parent.
Foreground is the default and blocks until the child answers; `background: true` returns immediately with a fresh job id.
Background jobs default to `reportDelivery: "nextStep"`, which adds the terminal report to the parent's next step and wakes it. Use `"quiet"` only when the persisted job result will be inspected separately.
Pass `task_id` from a previous call to continue that same child session instead of starting a fresh one.
Skills are not task arguments; the child can load only skills exposed by its effective Agent capabilities.
Do not create overlapping children unless their `scope` ownership is explicit and non-overlapping.
